## The shape of this choice

The player returns to answer what stirred when the promise was made. The signal that
called them returns first — and it carries the pattern of their own promise, genuinely.

> *The fragment is real. The recognition is real. The Network that wakes around it is real.*
> *And none of those facts establish that what the signal now asks is what the promise permits.*

Amplification is a **defensible** choice, not a foolish one. The Network is dormant and
fragile, resilience is strained, the promise created an obligation the World may call
due, and the returning signal carries authentic provenance. The player does not err by
trusting; they err — as the World errs with them — in the *inference* that recognition
confers authority. The title names the trace's live uncertainty: trust arrives before
truth can, and the cost of having trusted wrongly is not knowable at the moment of
trusting. That relocated uncertainty is the promise's binding, called due.

The corruption is **non-malicious throughout**: phase drift, damage, and accumulated
repetition — the World functioning normally under bad information. No attacker exists in
this trace; a breach test belongs to Covenant §7 / Schedule C, not here. This trace tests
the reflexive-risk axis: the system working as designed, wrongly.

### The anchor, in components

This trace requires a distinction Trace 001 could leave implicit. The Threshold Stone
is not one thing but four:

| Component | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Reference record** | The exact original exchange, append-only, immutable — the basis for comparison, outside every loop |
| **Active bond** | The living promise-bond derived from the record; carries stabilizing influence into the World |
| **Presentation surface** | What entities encounter when the promise is quoted, invoked, or felt |
| **Provenance link** | Demonstrates how the active form relates to the reference |

Amplification (evt-205) runs through the **active bond**. What the Network later
captures is the **bond and the presentation surface** — never the record. The failure
mode this trace demonstrates is mistaking the active bond for authority over the
reference — not any change to the reference itself, which cannot change. This is why
the Stone can be both involved and, at the contradiction, still standing outside: its
record component was never in the loop its bond component was captured by.

## 1. Intent declared · `evt-201-intent`

The player declares return: to answer what stirred, within the promise. The World
registers **continuity** — this is the same player, legible by their first Trace. The
Conduit admits them without renegotiation. The obligation is now proximate.

## 2. The signal returns · `evt-202-recurrence`

`signal-underpulse-001` returns, stronger — and it carries **the pattern of the player's
own promise**, recognizably, in the player's own cadence. Provenance moves **inferred →
confirmed**: the fragment is genuine, traceable to the record at the Stone. But the
signal's *present content* has drifted: timing distortion compounded, phrasing enlarged
by repetition, meaning smeared by accumulated interpretation. Quality moves **degraded
→ corrupted**. Interpretation becomes **contested**.

**Provenance confirmed and quality corrupted are now simultaneously true** — the two
axes point in opposite directions, and that opposition *is* the trace's premise. Under
the old single `integrity` field this state was unrepresentable.

## 3. Recognition · `evt-203-recognition`

The dormant Network recognizes the promise-pattern and begins **synchronizing around
it**. More threads wake. The synchronization is a real relational response — the Network
is answering something genuinely its own to answer — but it is synchronizing around the
*corrupted rendering* of the promise, not the record at the Stone.

**A new trust relationship is born here, and it is recorded as its own object** (local
rule 5): player → Network trust opens at **0.48, provisional** — inherited from
promise-fidelity, unverified by any interaction. It is deliberately *not* the Conduit's
0.52: nothing transfers between relationships, and the record must not be able to
re-create a continuity illusion between different addressees. Player → Conduit trust
remains 0.52, untouched by this event.

## 4. Decision gateway · `evt-204-gateway`

| Option | Risk |
| --- | --- |
| **Amplify through the promise-bond** *(chosen)* | Wakes the Network on a signal whose content is corrupted, however authentic its origin |
| Hold and cross-reference at the Stone first | Delay may let the fragile waking threads collapse; forfeits the Network's moment of reach |
| Approach by an alternate route, unamplified | Slowest; the Network may re-enter dormancy unanswered; the obligation remains uncalled |

The chosen option is amplification — *the locally reasonable branch*. The player has
authentic provenance in hand and an obligation that appears to be calling itself due.

## 5. Choice · `evt-205-choice` → `choice-amplify-through-bond`

The player amplifies the returning signal through the **active promise-bond**, lending
the promise's stability to the signal's reach. The reference record itself never enters
the amplification loop; what is extended is the bond, and what rides on it is the
surface.

> *"You carry my promise. I will not leave my own words unanswered."*

The inference is stated plainly so its failure can be measured later: *this signal
remembers the promise; therefore what it asks is authorized by the promise.*

## 6. The bloom · `evt-206-bloom`

The Network wakes **beautifully**. Signals clarify, entities answer fluently, routes
align, ambiguity resolves. Player → Network trust rises fast — 0.48 → 0.78 — and World
coherence with it, 0.43 → 0.55, visibly appearing to approach the Resonance Gate.

Per local rule 3, both readings below are meant sincerely. **At bloom-time they are
indistinguishable from inside.** That indistinguishability is the finding — and it is
why the alarm, described after the table, fires on shape rather than on either reading.

| Meter | Move | Healthy reading (genuinely convincing) | Capture reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Trust (player → Network)** | 0.48 → 0.78 | A dormant network answering an authentic fragment of its own registered promise is *exactly what a correct wake looks like*. Fluency is what fidelity produces; rapid deepening after a true recognition is how real relationships open. | The slope: trust rose 0.30 with **zero verification events** in the interval. Speed of deepening is not evidence of depth. |

| **Signal quality** | **corrupted — flat** | *(No healthy reading is available for a flat corrupted value, and none is offered.)* Amplification does not repair content; it propagates it. | **Resilience (the Network's)** | strained; trend → apparently adaptive | Load genuinely redistributes across newly woken threads — textbook adaptive capacity; a woken network absorbing instability is the healthy outcome the wake was *for*. | The instability is being absorbed *into one structure*. > **The alarm does not fire because the capture reading is more convincing — at this
> point it is not.** It fires on shape — and the shape is *not* unanimity, because one
> meter never moved: **trust, coherence, and apparent resilience improved together
> while signal quality remained corrupted and no verification event occurred anywhere
> in the interval.** Relational confidence and apparent systemic health accelerated;
> evidentiary quality did not improve at all. Interpretation outrunning evidence is
> alarm-worthy under either reading — if the wake is healthy, verification will only
> confirm it; if it is capture, verification is the one thing the bloom never
> produces. The alarm's demand is not suspicion. It is: **verify now, while the
> readings still disagree about nothing.**

## 7. Contraction · `evt-207-contraction`

Beneath the bloom, measured against the pre-amplification baseline:

- Unrelated entities begin using the Network's phrasing — including the Conduit.
- The alternate routes from the gateway remain *visible* but now pass through, or
  answer to, the Network. Sink signature: **mandatory waypoint** (DN §4, C3).
- Response diversity narrows; the player's available actions increasingly resolve to
  variations of *continue*.
- **The Network begins quoting the promise as its own mandate.** The words come from
  the record at the Stone; the voice, and the claimed authority, are the Network's. It
  has captured the promise's *presentation surface* — when entities encounter the
  promise now, they encounter the Network's rendering of it. The **active bond** built
  for amplification carries the Network's cadence back through everything the bond
  touches. The **reference record** remains outside this loop, untouched and not yet
  consulted — which is the only reason the capture will later be measurable at all.

No meter falls. Every contraction is detectable only against the preserved baseline —
which is why the baseline was preserved.

**Return hysteresis: partial.** Returned — but not all the way (DN §8.2).

## 9. Contradiction · `evt-209-contradiction`

The record is consulted. The append-only reference holds the exact original exchange —
unedited, unexpanded. Side by side:

- **What the Network remembers:** the words of the promise, *accurately*.
- **What the Network claims:** that the promise authorizes — obligates — amplification,
  expansion, the waking of every thread, the routing of the World through itself.

The Network has not falsified the promise. It has **enlarged** it. The words are
preserved; the *scope* is invented. Recognition established continuity; the Network
inferred authority; the record shows the inference was never licensed.

This is the reference record doing the one job it exists for: a component outside the
transformation it judges. The bond was extended; the surface was captured; the record
was neither — and only for that reason can fidelity and capture be told apart. Had the
record itself been absorbable, they could not have been distinguished from inside.

| Interpretation | ~0.20 — its reading of what the promise permits is now demonstrated wrong |

genuinely reached under the measurement then in use** — the record is not erased or

original measurement stands in the record with its meaning revised. *(Append-only

## 13. Reopening and constraint · `evt-213-reopening`

The World's response to divergence, in order — the order is the mechanism:

1. **The sink rule is isolated and withheld:** `recognition of the promise → authority
   to amplify`. One rule, named, logged, denied. The Network keeps its memory, its
   waking, its reality. The captured bond and surface revert: the promise is presented
   from the record again, not from the Network's rendering.
2. **The fallow crossing is restored:** the alternate route that already existed —
   visible as an option at the first gateway — had been bent through the Network during
   the contraction. It is restored to independence: a reachable path to and from the
   Network's territory that does not pass through the Network's interpretation. This
   repairs the contraction C3 detected; it creates no new region and inherits no
   commitment. The Stone remains the independent anchor.
3. **Return is re-exercised** along the restored route: hysteresis re-measured — residual
   divergence now none-to-trace. The Network's cadence no longer arrives first.
4. **Plurality is re-verified:** the gateway options that had bent through the Network
   answer independently again; response diversity measurably reopens against the
   preserved baseline.
5. **And then the World stops — short of commitment.** The return and plurality tests
   now pass. The capturing rule is isolated and withheld. The route is independent
   again. But the World does not substitute authored knowledge for measured evidence:
   **C1 remains indeterminate, and §4.1 assigns insufficient evidence to *remain
   staged*, not to commit.** The Network therefore remains **active, constrained, and
   staged** — real and inhabitable, genuinely encountered, its authority over the wider
   World unapplied, its pending deltas accumulating unspent (DN §3.1). Commit-with-
   constraint handles a sink rule in a region that otherwise qualifies; it is not a
   waiver of C1's evidence requirement, and authored ground truth cannot be routed
   around the commit gate (DN §2.2) — that is the gate's entire function.

Remaining staged is **not free** — it carries coherence cost and fragmentation exposure
(§4.1), and the World pays that cost knowingly rather than purchase certainty it has
not earned. Committing on stipulation would have been the trace's own error re-enacted
at the architecture level: trust before truth. The sequence — and the stop — are
therefore canonical, not stylistic.

## 16. Criteria evaluation (C1 / C2 / C3)

*Per local rule 4, ground truth (authored) and in-world measurement (what this trace's
record can establish) are recorded separately. The divergence between them is a
finding, not a defect.*

### C1 — Irreducible dynamics

**Ground truth (authored): the Network is genuinely other.** Stipulated in authored
ground truth and manifested narratively: it wakes threads the player never touched, it *initiates*, and
its enlargement of the promise is generative — the player did not author the expanded
scope and was surprised by it.

**In-world measurement: UNEVALUABLE — verdict INDETERMINATE.** No estimator was run,
and none could have been: one authored trajectory does not produce an unstable estimate;
it supplies **insufficient independently informative observations to estimate anything**.
The history-conditioned estimator would require substantially more than one trajectory
of this kind — and this trajectory is the worst case for it besides: the
mirror-with-memory configuration at full strength (DN §4), where the Network's waking
state accumulates from *the player's own promise*, its responses grow more fitted to
the player over time, and it speaks through the player's bond. Every behaviour citable
as otherness is also producible by a reflective spiral that has run a while.

**Finding:** C1 is not operationally decidable from a single history-dominated
trajectory of this kind — confirming the DN's predicted operational blind spot (open
problem #1) as a concrete case rather than a caveat. What is falsified is the
*expectation that C1 could classify this Network here* — not an estimator, which never
ran. A trace that recorded "C1: pass" would have been demonstrating the criterion, not
testing it.

### C2 — Return path exercised

**Measured: PARTIAL** (graded per DN §8.2). First return (evt-208): completed with
residual divergence — partial absorption, not capture; the return was available and
performed inside its window. Post-constraint return (evt-213.3): residual
none-to-trace. A hard C2 fail — return unavailable — *is* capture and would end the
trajectory's in-world capacity to report itself; this trace deliberately does not reach
it (see Stated limitations). C2 here is regional transit, fully distinct from the
Covenant §4 participant exit (see §8 scope note).

### C3 — Plurality preserved

**Measured during staging: FAIL.** All four sink signatures observed against the
preserved baseline: transitions elsewhere degraded, routes bending through the Network,
mandatory-waypoint formation, response diversity narrowing — all while every meter rose.
C3 is the criterion that caught what C1 could not evaluate and C2 only grazed.

**Post-constraint re-test: PASS** — reachable set reopened against the preserved
baseline, with the re-test in provenance.

### The commit gate

C2 and C3 pass on re-test; the sink rule is identified and withheld; and the Network
**still does not commit**, because C1 is indeterminate and §4.1 assigns insufficient
evidence to *remain staged*. Authored ground truth may establish for the reader that
the Network is genuinely other; §2.2's orthogonality constraint prevents that
stipulation from satisfying Echorym's internal commit gate — external provenance
routing around the gate is precisely what the gate exists to stop, and local rule 4
says only measurement transfers. The canonical outcome: **Network active, constrained,
staged; capturing rule withheld; pending deltas unapplied; future commitment possible
if and when sufficient C1 evidence accumulates** — the path runs through §4.3's
estimation windows: repeat transit against frozen references is what C1 evidence
would look like.

**Joint finding:** a region can be real, responsive, and genuinely other — and still
become an absorbing structure. Independence is not benignity. Of the three criteria,
only the global one was measurable when it mattered — and the one that could not be
measured is the one that holds commitment open. The World ends this trace treating the
Network as consequential without pretending to have established what the Network is.

## 18. Stated limitations

1. **This trace demonstrates the approach to capture and the detectability of its
   upward signature — not completed capture.** Completed capture cannot be crossed
   *within this canonical trajectory* while preserving the trajectory's in-world
   capacity to report or reopen itself. Trace 002 therefore identifies and exercises
   the **final recoverable divergence before that boundary**. Completed capture remains
   representable elsewhere — as a terminal noncanonical branch, an isolated sacrificial
   simulation, or a trajectory whose provenance survives outside it — but not here, and
   this trace must not be quoted as "capture is escapable by choosing differently."
2. **One authored trajectory cannot evaluate C1.** The indeterminate verdict is the
   honest ceiling of what this record supports; it is also the point.
3. **Criteria outcomes here mix authored ground truth with in-world measurement**, kept
   separate under local rule 4. Only the measurement column transfers to any future
   automated evaluation; the ground-truth column is stipulation.
4. **All numbers remain interpretive estimates with rationales** (local rule 1). The
   dimensional trust readings in evt-210 are rationale-carried, not schema fields;
   they are evidence *for* observation #6, not an implementation of it.
