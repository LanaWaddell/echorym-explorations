# Sample Scenario

## Entry

The player arrives at the First Conduit with a declared intent: to find a way through without breaking the signal that called them.

The conduit answers with a pulse that feels almost patterned. A second signal arrives underneath it, quieter and older, but its provenance is unclear.

## World response

The conduit opens only partway. A dormant trust network stirs nearby, not fully awake and not fully silent.

## Decision gateway

The player chooses one:

1. Stabilize the conduit now using the partial signal.
2. Wait and listen for a cleaner signal.
3. Offer a memory or promise as an anchor before moving.

## Consequence sketch

- Stabilizing now may raise resilience but risk signal distortion.
- Waiting may improve signal integrity but lower short-term conduit stability.
- Offering an anchor may increase trust but bind the player to a remembered obligation.

## Memory

Whatever the player chooses becomes the first trace the world keeps about them.
