# Glossary

## Anchor

A stabilizing point in the world that preserves local coherence, memory continuity, or relational orientation.

## Coherence

The degree to which world state, memory, signal, intent, and relationship remain mutually intelligible over time.

## Coherence gate

A threshold where the world changes interpretive mode. Gates mark pattern emergence, resonance, topological lock, phase travel, or unified field conditions.

## Consequence

The effect of an action across world state, relationships, trust, memory, signal integrity, and resilience.

## Conduit

A passage or relational channel through which intent, memory, signal, or world-state change can move.

## Dormant trust network

A latent relational structure that can be reactivated, repaired, corrupted, or lost depending on choices and signals.

## Intent field

The declared, inferred, and contextual intention surrounding a player, entity, or world response.

## Memory trace

A durable record of a meaningful event, including its source, affected entities, emotional or relational weight, and downstream consequences.

## Resilience

The ability of a player, entity, relationship, or world region to absorb disruption and retain or recover coherence.

## Signal integrity

The reliability, clarity, provenance, and corruption state of information moving through the world.

## Trust

A relationship-state variable shaped by consistency, vulnerability, repair, deception, uncertainty, and memory.
