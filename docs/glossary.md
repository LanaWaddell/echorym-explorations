# Glossary

## Anchor

A stabilizing point in the world that preserves local coherence, memory continuity, or relational orientation.

## Coherence

The degree to which world state, memory, signal, intent, and relationship remain mutually intelligible over time.

## Coherence gate

A threshold marker for a qualitative change in world interpretation or possibility. Gates are not reward tiers or fixed level locks.

## Consequence

The effect of an action across world-state, relationships, trust, memory, signal integrity, and resilience.

## Conduit

A passage, threshold, or relational channel through which intent, memory, signal, or world-state change can move.

## Content

Any authored or generated material presented inside or around the system, including text, images, dialogue, logs, scenes, rules, and descriptions. In Echorym, content is treated as a signal that may affect coherence, trust, resilience, memory, and world evolution. It is not the primary purpose of the system.

## Decision gateway

A moment where a player chooses under uncertainty and the consequence affects relationship, trust, coherence, signal integrity, resilience, memory, or world-state.

## Dormant trust network

A latent relational structure that can be reactivated, repaired, corrupted, or lost depending on choices and signals.

## Echorym

An adaptive coherence-world system centered on relationship under uncertainty.

## Intent field

The declared, inferred, contextual, and residual intention surrounding a player, entity, action, or world response.

## Mechanics layer

The design layer that defines how change is represented. It includes coherence gates, trust changes, intent fields, signal integrity, resilience, decision gateways, and consequence tracking.

## Memory trace

A durable record of a meaningful event, including its source, affected entities, emotional or relational weight, and downstream consequences.

## Prototype 0: The First Conduit

The smallest living Echorym loop: a player enters with intent, the world responds, the player chooses under uncertainty, trust, coherence, signal integrity, and resilience change, and the world remembers and evolves.

## Research layer

The design layer that holds theoretical inputs, external references, questions, and interpretations. Research informs design but does not automatically become project authority.

## Resilience

The ability of a player, entity, relationship, or world region to absorb disruption and retain or recover coherence.

## Signal

Any information-bearing element that can be interpreted by the player, world, entity, experiment, or reviewer. Content is one class of signal.

## Signal integrity

The reliability, clarity, provenance, continuity, and corruption state of information moving through the world.

## Simulation/experiment layer

The design layer that defines how Echorym behavior can be tested, replayed, compared, and evaluated. It includes schemas, sample data, trajectories, run configurations, evaluation notes, and reproducibility practices.

## State / action / consequence / coherence trajectory

A trace format that records initial state, action or choice, resulting consequence, and coherence-related change over time. It replaces reward-centered framing for Echorym experiments.

## Trust

A relationship-state variable shaped by consistency, vulnerability, repair, deception, uncertainty, memory, and signal integrity.

## World layer

The design layer that defines what exists and what can remember. It includes places, entities, anchors, dormant trust networks, conduits, world-state, memory traces, and relationship history.
