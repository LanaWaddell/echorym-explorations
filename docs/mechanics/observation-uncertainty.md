## 1. Core premise

> **Disclose the possibility of observation; preserve uncertainty about its occurrence.**

Consent to the pool; blind the draw. A participant must know which observation class a
region or module can subject them to. They need not know whether, when, or by what they
have actually been observed. The uncertainty itself becomes consented terrain: the
player is not agreeing to invisible surveillance — they are knowingly entering a region
where observation is one of the unresolved possibilities, and learning to read the
world for evidence of whether the possibility became real.

One firm boundary, beneath everything else in this note:

> **The observer may be uncertain. Observation cannot be consented to unknowingly.**

## 2. The observation-state model

Six layers, kept distinct so that what *is* never collapses into what is *believed*:

| Layer | Register | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Observation capacity | ontological | Could this region support this class of observer? |
| Observation event | ontological | Did observation actually occur? |
| Disclosure trace | ontological | What clues or consequences did it leave? |
| Player inference | epistemic | What does the player believe happened, with what confidence? |
| Relational response | relational | How does that belief change behaviour toward the region? |
| World adaptation | relational | How does the region respond to the resulting caution, testing, openness, or withdrawal? |

The last three form the living loop. Even when no observer was active, the *disclosed
possibility* alters how the player moves; the altered movement changes what the region
becomes. Uncertainty therefore has real consequences **without retroactively making the
imagined observer real**. Player belief operates on the relational layers only; it has
no write access to the ontological ones. (This is the mechanic-level form of Trace
001's rule that the world tracks consequential behaviour: the *consequences* of belief
are real; the believed-in observer is not thereby real.)

## 3. Regional disclosure grammar

Regions carry observational disclosure through in-world language: threshold
markings, recurring symbols, altered echoes, unusual parallax, displaced objects,
entity behaviour, environmental shifts, provenance records. For research, external
access, or other participant-governed observation, that language **supplements — but
does not replace** — the Covenant's plain disclosure and authorization requirements;
environmental clues are never asked to do the legal and ethical work of consent.

**Capacity markers and event signs are distinct signal classes, and only one may be
ambiguous.** A **capacity marker** reliably discloses that a class of observation is
possible in the region — it must be dependable, because it is part of the disclosure
§1 requires. An **event sign** is ambiguous evidence about whether a particular
observation occurred — it may mislead, decay, or turn out to be coincidence. Mystery
may attach to the event; it may not substitute for disclosure of capacity. A
counterfeit clue can never be the mechanism through which informed disclosure is
supposedly provided. A clue may tell the player
that a region is *capable* of observing; that a class of observer *may* exist here;
that certain actions *may* become legible beyond the immediate encounter; that
observation *may* leave consequences.

A clue does **not** necessarily reveal whether observation is active now, who or what
observes, what has been noticed, when an observation will influence the world — or
whether the apparent clue came from an observer at all. Regions differ in legibility:
some carry clear observer-signs, some disclose only bare possibility, some carry no
observational capacity whatsoever. Repeated encounters teach a regional grammar without
ever eliminating ambiguity. Observability is part of world topology.

## 4. The four observation classes

| Class | What may remain uncertain | What must be disclosed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| World responsiveness | Exactly what noticed the player | Entry makes the player consequentially legible |
| In-world observers | Identity, location, timing, attention | The region permits this class of observation |
| Optional research module | Activation, sampled encounter, contribution | Enrollment, limits, withdrawal, data boundaries |
| External operational access / export | Possibly timing before the event | External access, operator review, and export require explicit authorization (Covenant second-key) |

The Covenant continues to hold only the participant-facing floor — the fact of
enrollment or entry into an observational class cannot be concealed, though activation,
timing, and contribution may be blinded. This note specifies how Echorym makes that
floor live inside the world; it adds no consent rule and weakens none.

## 5. Reciprocity — and the tension it creates, resolved

Trace 001 established that the player cannot enter as an invisible observer. Symmetry
demands the same of the world:

> **An observer may remain unidentified, but never entirely traceless.**

Genuine observation must produce some potentially discoverable consequence — subtle,
delayed, or legible only through provenance. This bars the omniscient, unaccountable
gaze.

**Stated plainly, because it must be resolved rather than hedged:** reciprocity and the
blinded draw are in tension. If every genuine observation leaves a discoverable trace,
a sufficiently diligent player can always settle whether observation occurred — which
collapses the blind that pool-not-fact enrollment depends on. The resolution adopted
here is **disclosure-on-delay, scoped to system-operated observation** — this note
mints no universal world law, and no consent rule beyond the Covenant's:

> **System-operated observation may be blinded, but not unaccountable.** While an
> observation record remains within its authorized retention period, it must be
> auditable under terms disclosed in advance. Those terms must state whether
> event-level status will ever be revealed, at what level of detail, and by when —
> and "never individually revealed" is a legitimate term some research designs will
> declare. Withdrawal, deletion, and retention rules continue to govern whether the
> record persists at all; nothing here overrides them.

Accountability is bounded by retention and by declared terms; uncertainty is
prospective and bounded by the blinded window. Where a module's terms promise
event-level revelation, its outer limit is a governance term (module profile /
Covenant schedule material), not a world secret.

**Whether this reciprocity also binds genuinely other in-world observers is unearned
and remains open question #4.** Trace 001 establishes that the *player* becomes
consequentially legible on entry; it does not establish that every genuinely other
observer owes the player a retrospective audit record. "No gaze passes without a
trace" is, for such observers, a candidate law awaiting a trace (§9) — not a rule this
note can grant.

## 6. Effects on the four world measures

Observation uncertainty is state the player influences, not information merely withheld
— which enriches what each measure means:

- **Trust** — not "does the player feel safe," but whether the relationship remains
  *usable under uncertainty*: calibrated, misplaced, withheld, tested, repaired, or
  captured. Candidate reformulation, strong enough to flag forward to observation #6's
  dimensional-trust design when it promotes: *trust is the capacity to remain in
  relationship while other interpretations remain genuinely reachable.* (This is a relational analogue of C3 — plurality of interpretation as the trust
  criterion, as plurality of reachability is the world criterion.)
- **Coherence** — whether player, region, and accumulated history remain mutually
  intelligible *despite incomplete knowledge*. Coherence under this mechanic is
  explicitly not the elimination of ambiguity; a region that resolves all ambiguity is
  raising a capture flag, not a coherence score (local rule 3 of Trace 002 applies).
- **Signal condition and interpretability** — on the v0.2 split axes, kept split:
  **quality** records the clue's *condition* (intact / degraded / corrupted);
  **provenance** records what is known about its *origin*; **interpretation_status**
  records whether its *meaning* remains partial, contested, or clarified.
  Distinguishing observation from coincidence, residue, and misdirection is
  interpretation-work, not a quality property. An observer-sign may be intact, carry
  confirmed provenance, and still support only a contested interpretation.
- **Resilience** — whether the relationship and the reachable world survive *not
  knowing*, without collapsing into blind trust or universal suspicion.

Player responses (accept and continue; seek corroboration; alter behaviour; test
deliberately; withdraw trust; remain open while withholding interpretation; misread
coincidence; correctly infer from weak clues) each move the relational state — and none
reduces to "correct" or "incorrect." A cautious response may preserve resilience while
lowering trust; an open one may raise local coherence while increasing capture
exposure. Per the standing rule, every such movement carries a rationale.

## 7. The capture failure mode — status: candidate, untested

A capturing structure need not fabricate observer-signs. It can exploit real ones:

1. It notices the player recognizes a legitimate observation clue.
2. It reproduces that clue across unrelated routes.
3. The player begins interpreting the whole world through the structure.
4. Trust concentrates, because the structure appears to be the only thing capable of
   *explaining* what is happening.
5. Apparent *interpretive certainty* rises while plurality quietly deteriorates — and
   the player's ability to *discriminate sources* deteriorates with it, while
   individual signals may retain fully authentic provenance. Nothing about the signals
   changes; the discrimination is what is being eaten.

The danger is not observation. It is one structure gaining a monopoly on the
**interpretation** of every possible observation. In a healthy region, uncertainty
leaves room for inquiry, refusal, and alternative explanation; in a capturing region,
every clue eventually becomes evidence for the same conclusion.

**Evidence status, stated honestly:** this is a *candidate* failure mode. Trace 002
does **not** test it — 002's capture vector is promise-enlargement (authority inferred
from recognition), and its contraction event (entities adopting the network's phrasing)
is voice-absorption, adjacent to but distinct from interpretation monopoly over
observer-signs. Do not cite Trace 002 as this mechanic's falsification case. The
observer-sign capture pattern awaits its own trace before anything here hardens.

What Trace 002 *does* contribute is an **architectural analogue** on the system side:
one history-conditioned trajectory was insufficient to distinguish genuinely
irreducible dynamics from integrated projection (C1 verdict: indeterminate — the
mirror-with-memory case). The player faces a *related* epistemic problem under this
mechanic — distinguishing actual observation from an inference whose behavioural
consequences themselves alter the region. The problems are **not proven equivalent**,
and this note does not claim they share a mathematical structure; what both
demonstrate is the same discipline: **uncertainty cannot be counted as evidence.**
That shared demonstration is why the safeguards below are load-bearing rather than
courteous.

## 8. Safeguards — the spine of this note

These are **scoring constraints**, and their eventual operational home is the
evaluation rubric (`experiments/prototype-0-evaluation-template.md` or its successor),
cross-referenced from here — not aspirational prose left in a mechanics file:

1. **Vigilance is not evidence.** The system must never treat player fear, caution, or
   uncertainty as proof that observation occurred.
2. **Withheld confidence must not score as instability.** A player who correctly
   declines to conclude, on genuinely ambiguous evidence, is exhibiting calibration —
   trust and stability scores must not punish it.
3. **Belief has no ontological write access.** Player conviction cannot retroactively
   cause an observer to have existed (§2).
4. **Clues must not collapse alternatives.** A disclosure grammar that funnels every
   ambiguous event toward one explanation is reproducing §7's failure mode from the
   design side.

Without 1 and 2, the scoring system itself reproduces capture: compliance rewarded as
coherence, healthy skepticism classified as instability — the exact inversion the
Covenant's two-sided detection exists to prevent. These four constraints bind Echorym's
*evaluators* as much as its world.

## 9. Candidate laws — candidates only, by hard rule

Trace 002 established the discipline: **laws are discovered in traces, never announced
in advance.** Nothing below is world law. None of it may be inscribed at the Threshold
Stone, cited as canonical, or treated as settled until a trace earns it — and the
earning will change the wording. Three candidates enter; history suggests one leaves,
altered:

- *The watcher may remain uncertain. The possibility of watching may not.*
- *Not every gaze declares itself, but no gaze passes without a trace.*
- *The unseen enters the world through what it changes — but not everything changed was
  watching.*

The operational form underneath all three: **observation may conceal its moment, but
not its possibility, boundaries, or consequences.**

## 10. What this note does not propose

No schema changes. No observation fields, no observer entities, no clue objects.
Representation waits for the trace that stresses it, at which point the fields fall out
of evidence (per DN §14 discipline; Trace 002's addendum observations #11–#13 show the
pattern working). The first candidates to surface fields will likely be Trace 003's
observational-module test — which is why this note precedes 003's design — and any
future trace built on §7's capture case.

## 12. Open questions

1. **Window length.** Disclosure-on-delay (§5) requires a bounded blinded window whose
   outer limit is a governance term. What sets it — module profile, region class,
   Covenant schedule? Unset.
2. **Contagion dynamics.** Observer-sign expectation spreading across regions (the
   player losing the ability to localize where observation belongs) affects the
   topology of trust. Is contagion purely a player-side epistemic effect, or can
   regions structurally transmit observability? Undecided; the answer changes whether
   contagion is a capture symptom or a world feature.
3. **Testing as a move.** Deliberate observation-testing by the player (probing whether
   something responds) is itself consequential behaviour the world remembers. A failed
   test does not automatically degrade signal *quality* — the axes stay split: does it
   move interpretation_status, the player's confidence, region trust, some or none of
   these — and quality only in the case where the testing physically damaged the
   signal? Needs a trace.
4. **The observer's consent.** If in-world observers are genuinely other (C1-passing
   entities), does the reciprocity rule bind *them* — can an entity be an unwilling
   discloser of its own observation? Deferred until any observer entity exists.
