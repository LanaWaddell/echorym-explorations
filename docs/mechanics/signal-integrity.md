# Signal Integrity

## Purpose

Signal integrity describes whether information can be trusted, traced, interpreted, and carried without harmful distortion.

## Starter dimensions

- **Clarity**: Is the signal understandable?
- **Provenance**: Is the signal source known?
- **Continuity**: Does the signal remain stable across time or relays?
- **Corruption**: Has the signal been degraded, altered, spoofed, or contaminated?
- **Resolution**: Is the signal detailed enough to support action?
- **Cross-checkability**: Can the signal be compared against memory or other evidence?

## Consequences

Low signal integrity can create false choices, broken trust, fragmented memory, or unstable world-state transitions. High signal integrity can make repair, coordination, and resonance possible.

## Prototype use

Prototype 0 should mark at least one moment where a player must act before signal integrity is complete.
