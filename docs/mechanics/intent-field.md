# Intent Field

## Purpose

The intent field captures why an action is taken, how that intent is perceived, and how uncertainty changes interpretation.

## Layers

- **Declared intent**: What the player or entity says they are trying to do.
- **Inferred intent**: What other entities or systems believe is happening.
- **Contextual intent**: What the world state makes more or less plausible.
- **Residual intent**: What remains unresolved after action and consequence.

## Prototype use

In Prototype 0, a player enters with intent. The world should respond to both declared intent and surrounding uncertainty. The scenario should preserve ambiguity where appropriate instead of explaining everything immediately.
