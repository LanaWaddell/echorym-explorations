# Resilience Model

## Purpose

Resilience describes the ability to absorb disruption, retain identity, and recover coherence after stress.

## Starter dimensions

- **Absorption**: How much disruption can be taken without collapse?
- **Adaptation**: Can the system change without losing continuity?
- **Redundancy**: Are there alternate paths, memories, or relationships?
- **Repair capacity**: Can damage be acknowledged and restored?
- **Learning**: Does the system become wiser after strain?

## Relationship to coherence

Coherence can be fragile without resilience. Resilience can preserve meaning during incoherent phases, allowing the world to recover rather than simply fail.

## Prototype use

Prototype 0 should track whether a choice strengthens, weakens, or reveals resilience in the player, entity, or local world region.
