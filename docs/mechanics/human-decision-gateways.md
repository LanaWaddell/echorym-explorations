# Human Decision Gateways

## Purpose

Human decision gateways mark moments where the player must choose under uncertainty and where the choice should produce meaningful consequence.

## Gateway properties

- The available information is incomplete.
- The player can declare or revise intent.
- More than one value may be at stake.
- Consequences affect trust, coherence, signal integrity, resilience, memory, or world state.
- The world remembers the choice.

## Prototype use

Prototype 0 should contain one primary decision gateway at the First Conduit. It should be small enough to play by hand and rich enough to reveal the core loop.

## Design caution

A gateway is not a quiz with one correct answer. It is a pressure point where values, uncertainty, and relationship become visible.
