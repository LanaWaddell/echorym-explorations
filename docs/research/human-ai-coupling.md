# Human-AI Coupling

## Relevance

Echorym may use AI systems as design partners, simulation assistants, world-response agents, evaluators, or memory tools. The core question is how human intent and machine interpretation interact under uncertainty.

## Design interests

- Intent declaration and misinterpretation
- Trust calibration between human and AI systems
- Memory support without replacing human judgment
- Reflection loops that improve clarity rather than optimize for engagement
- Provenance and review of AI-generated material

## Prototype caution

Prototype 0 does not require an AI runtime. The loop can be tested manually first, then instrumented later.
