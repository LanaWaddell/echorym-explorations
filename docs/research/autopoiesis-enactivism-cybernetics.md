# Autopoiesis, Enactivism, and Cybernetics

## Relevance

These traditions offer language for self-maintaining systems, embodied sense-making, feedback, control, adaptation, and circular causality.

## Possible design links

- A world that maintains coherence through feedback.
- Players who enact meaning through choice rather than consume static content.
- Entities that preserve identity while adapting to perturbation.
- Memory and signal loops that change future perception.

## Open questions

- What would count as self-maintenance in Echorym?
- How does the world distinguish adaptation from corruption?
- Can coherence be measured without flattening lived meaning?
