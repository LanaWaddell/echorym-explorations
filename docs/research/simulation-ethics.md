# Simulation Ethics

## Purpose

Echorym may eventually simulate trust, memory, relationships, uncertainty, and adaptive world-state. These are sensitive concepts that should be handled carefully.

## Starter principles

- Do not present simulated trust as real consent or real emotional authority.
- Keep human review in the loop for claims about behavior, psychology, or relationships.
- Preserve provenance for generated or imported material.
- Avoid optimizing for manipulation, compulsion, or dependency.
- Make uncertainty visible where it matters.

## Prototype use

Prototype 0 should stay small and transparent. It should test structural consequences, not attempt to model a full person.
