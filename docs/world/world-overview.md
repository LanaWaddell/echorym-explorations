# World Overview

## Working premise

Echorym is a world where signals, relationships, memory, and coherence are part of the environment itself. Choices do not merely unlock content. They change what the world can remember, trust, transmit, and become.

## Core world concerns

- Relationship under uncertainty
- Dormant trust networks
- Memory traces that shape future interpretation
- Signal integrity across damaged or ambiguous channels
- Resilience after disruption
- Coherence gates that alter what is possible

## Early world shape

The first prototype begins near a conduit: a threshold where intent enters the world and receives a response. The world is not fully explained. It should feel responsive, partial, and alive enough to remember.

## Open questions

- What does the world want to preserve?
- What forms can memory take?
- Which entities can read coherence?
- What happens when trust networks wake incorrectly?
